Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026 · Majors Hunter Group

Majors Hunter Group ("MHG," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use, store, and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to majorshuntergroup.com and its subpages (the "Site"), to inquiries you submit through the Site, and to email, phone, and text communications with us that result from those inquiries.

1. Who We Are

Majors Hunter Group is a real estate, lending, and investment firm. We are the controller of personal information collected through the Site. Contact details are in Section 14.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you submit the contact form or contact us directly, we collect:

  • Identifiers and contact information: name, email address, company, and phone number (if you provide it).
  • Inquiry details: the message and any other details you choose to write.
  • Communications: the content of emails, calls, and text messages you send us, and our replies.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and embedded services may receive your IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps.

Information we do not seek. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, government ID numbers, or payment card details through the Site. Please do not include them in a form. We do not knowingly collect precise geolocation or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate potential business relationships.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the Site, detect spam and abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it.
  • To keep business records and comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and Similar Jurisdictions)

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we process personal information based on: your consent; our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries, running and securing the Site, and developing our business, balanced against your rights; steps taken at your request before entering into a relationship; and compliance with legal obligations.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Vendors that process data on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection obligations. Currently these include: Vercel (Site hosting and delivery); MHG Command, our internal CRM, hosted on Vercel and Supabase (storing and routing inquiries); LeadConnector / GoHighLevel (CRM, internal text and email alerts); Google Workspace (email); Google Fonts (font delivery); and email and telephony carriers used to communicate with you. Vendors may change over time, and we will keep this list current.
  • Professional advisers. Attorneys, accountants, and other advisers bound by duties of confidentiality.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MHG, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.
  • With your direction. Any other disclosure you request or authorize.

No mobile information (including phone numbers) is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may use technologies necessary to deliver and secure the Site. You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. The Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, but we honor the Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of sale or sharing.

Spam protection. Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell real visitors from bots. Turnstile runs in the background and may collect limited technical signals from your browser and device, such as IP address and browser characteristics, to make that determination. Cloudflare processes this data under its Turnstile Privacy Addendum and its Privacy Policy.

7. Text and Email Communications

We contact you by text or email only about your inquiry or a relationship you have with us, or where you have consented. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Emails we send for business development include a way to ask us to stop, and we honor requests promptly and within the time required by law. Our messaging follows the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, CTIA guidelines, and the CAN-SPAM Act.

8. Data Storage and International Transfers

Information is stored on systems operated by our service providers, primarily in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data-protection laws than your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by our providers.

9. Data Retention

  • Inquiry and contact records that do not lead to a business relationship: generally up to 3 years from our last interaction, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Records tied to a transaction, investment, loan, or contract: for the life of the relationship plus the period required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes (generally up to 7 years).
  • Server and security logs: generally 30 to 90 days, unless needed to investigate an incident.

We may keep information longer when required by law or needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, access limited to people who need it, and credential-based access controls on our business systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we hold about you and receive a portable copy.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising or profiling.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not use it beyond what is needed to respond to you.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority or state attorney general.

To exercise a right, email info@majorshuntergroup.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity by matching details you give us to the information we hold. We respond within 45 days (or sooner where the law requires), and may extend once by another 45 days with notice. You may use an authorized agent, and we may require proof of authorization. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying with the word "Appeal," and we will respond within 60 days.
